● SQL functions, CASE and COALESCE – String/date/numeric functions, CASE expressions, COALESCE/NULLIF, CAST, conditional
 aggregation
● Set operations (UNION, UNION ALL, INTERSECT, EXCEPT) – Duplicate handling, column compatibility, performance of UNION vs
 UNION ALL
